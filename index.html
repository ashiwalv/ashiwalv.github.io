> CURRENT_FOCUS_

Advancing industrial automation through applied AI.

Senior Research Scientist and Software Architect working at the intersection of generative AI, multi-agent systems and industrial automation.

I research and develop practical AI methods and software architectures for industrial automation. My work focuses on applying generative and agentic AI across the control-engineering lifecycle—from system specification and control-code generation to testing and deployment. I also explore agentic solutions for commissioning and troubleshooting industrial drives, as well as modular and distributed control-software architectures based on IEC 61499 and IEC 61131. The goal is to make industrial engineering more intelligent, efficient and dependable.
